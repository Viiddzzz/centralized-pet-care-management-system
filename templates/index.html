Welcome to Centralized Pet Care System

An Centralized Pet Care System is a web-based or mobile application designed to centralize and streamline pet care, health tracking, and administrative tasks for owners, vets, and pet-related businesses. These platforms offer features like digital health records, appointment scheduling, vaccination reminders, and, in some cases,IoT-enabled health monitoring.

Property managers and hospitality businesses often struggle to handle pets in a structured way. Without a proper system, tracking pet records, verifying vaccination details, enforcing pet policies, and managing pet-related incidents can become confusing and time-consuming. This creates risks such as policy violations, liability issues, and poor experiences for residents or guests.
